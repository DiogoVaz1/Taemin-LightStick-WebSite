# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Taemin fans (K-pop fandom), primarily at home on desktop or laptop, building and sharing lightshow sequences for their SHINee/Taemin lightstick. They care about getting results quickly — import a video, sync the lights, share with friends. Secondary context: occasionally used on mobile at events to control the lightstick live.

## Product Purpose

LightStickWaves lets fans control their Taemin lightstick from the browser via Web Bluetooth — no app install required. The product has four core modes: a live controller (color + speed), a lightshow studio (sync light patterns to video), a personal lightshow library, and a community hub to browse and share shows. Free, fan-made, built with obsessive care.

## Brand Personality

Minimal · focused · functional — a precision tool made with obvious love. Not a flashy fan site. The product is the respect you show the craft.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / dashboard look (Stripe-blue, enterprise-clean, cold metrics UI, startup neutrals)
- Music-streaming clone aesthetic (Spotify green, card grid, generic dark mode)
- Busy anime/fandom site (starfields, kawaii, ornamental noise)

## Design Principles

1. **Tool-first** — every page exists to complete a task; chrome earns its place.
2. **Dark by nature** — the interface lives in concert darkness; the dark theme is not a mode, it's the identity.
3. **Color is functional** — purple/pink/cyan are semantic (brand, action, status), not decoration.
4. **Speed of use** — primary actions within one tap/click; no wizard flows for simple tasks.
5. **Quiet confidence** — no hero metrics, no self-congratulating copy, no decorative panels; the product speaks through what it does.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard navigable. Mobile-usable for quick controller tasks. Reduced motion respected.
